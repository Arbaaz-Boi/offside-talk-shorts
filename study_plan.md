# 20-channel study plan (Nov 9, 2026)

Source: study of the top 10 Shorts of 20 channels with 20+ Shorts over 1M views (200 videos). Full report: https://claude.ai/artifact/DgfGAPbr4SkMEXmAMSECae

## What the study showed (apply to every plan Short)
- "Inside your body" 3D is the strongest lane (about half of the 100M+ videos): teeth, ears, throat, skin, heart, gut. Slightly gross reveal, no gore.
- 25-35 s performs best (median 51M); keep 28-35 s.
- How / Why titles beat What if (median 54M / 66M vs 17M). Superlatives ("World's Most...", "Deadliest...") also win.
- Short titles (about 6 words), object-first ("Cotton Swab", "Wrong Pipe"), one emoji at the end.
- Most comments come from a personal choice/confession question (would you...? have you ever...?).
- Skip: weapon how-it-works, crime/tragedy news, footage clips, kids-only styling.
